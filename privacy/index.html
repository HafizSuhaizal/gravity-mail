Gravity Mail Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Gravity Mail does not ask for your name, email or any account, and keeps your game progress only on your own phone. The game shows optional video ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect device information to show and measure those ads, as explained below.

Who we are

Gravity Mail is a mobile game for Android and iPhone made by Mohd Hafiz Suhaizal Bin Ismail in Malaysia ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy or your data: suhaizaltechsolutions@gmail.com.

What the game keeps on your device

The game saves a small file on your phone so you can continue where you left off. It never leaves your phone and we cannot see it.

Saved on your phoneWhy
Levels unlocked, stars, best scoresTo keep your progress
Coins and shop items you own or useTo keep what you earned
Settings (sound, vibration, language, controls)To remember your choices
Daily free-coin countTo limit free coins to a few a day

We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics. We do not sell or share any data ourselves.

Ads by Google AdMob

The game uses Google AdMob to show video ads that you choose to watch, for example to continue a level or get free coins. To show, limit and measure these ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID, or Apple's ID for advertisers only if you allow tracking)
  • your IP address, used to estimate your general location
  • device and app details, such as phone model, operating system and the app's name
  • how you interact with ads, and crash or performance information about the ads

Google uses this as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. Ads may be personalised or non-personalised depending on your choices below.

Your choices

  • EU, UK and Switzerland: before any ad loads, the game shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time with the Privacy button in Settings.
  • iPhone: iOS asks whether Gravity Mail may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your ID for advertisers is not shared. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact menu depends on your phone).
  • No ads at all: ads only play when you tap a "watch ad" button. You can play every level without watching any.

Children

Gravity Mail is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Game data: deleting the app deletes your saved progress, coins and settings from your phone.
  • Ad data: Google keeps ad data under its own policy. You can manage it at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act, or the GDPR in the EU), you may ask what data is held about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Email us and we will reply within 21 days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Contact: suhaizaltechsolutions@gmail.com.
